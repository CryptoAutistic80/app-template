# Game Design Checklist

## Core Loop
- Player goal is obvious within 10 seconds.
- One primary action drives the loop.
- Risk/reward is clear and visible.
- Session length matches audience (short bursts for mobile).

## Progression
- Difficulty ramps in small, predictable steps.
- New elements are introduced one at a time.
- Failure teaches the player how to improve.

## Feedback
- Every action has immediate visual and audio feedback.
- Success is celebrated; failure is readable and fair.
- Scoring or progress is always visible.

## Controls
- Controls work with one hand when possible.
- Touch targets are large and forgiving.
- Latency between input and response feels instant.

## Content & Variety
- Variety changes the decision, not just the visuals.
- Repetition is interrupted by micro-goals or new mechanics.

## Retention & Motivation
- Short-term goals are explicit (next score, next level, daily goal).
- Long-term goals are framed early (unlock, mastery, challenge).

## Accessibility
- Color contrast is adequate.
- Audio cues have visual equivalents.
- Provide a low-motion option if heavy effects are used.
